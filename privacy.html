Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Sticky Notes ("the App") is made by Dynomite ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App uses and why.

Your notes

  • Your notes, checklists, tags and settings are stored on your device.
  • If iCloud is turned on, they sync through your private iCloud account (Apple CloudKit). We can't see or read them.
  • Widgets read your notes on your device through a shared app container. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Day Highlight is created on your device with Apple Intelligence. Your notes don't leave your device for it.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase to understand how the App is used and to fix problems:

  • Firebase Analytics collects usage data such as app opens, screens viewed, session length, device model, iOS version and approximate country.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports with device and app details.

Neither includes the content of your notes. This data is processed under Google's Firebase privacy terms.

Purchases

Sticky Notes Pro is sold through the App Store, so Apple handles payment and we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check your subscription status. It receives an anonymous app user ID and your App Store purchase history. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't have servers that store your notes.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Notes stay until you delete them. Deleting the App removes its data from your device; data in iCloud follows Apple's policies.
  • You can turn off iCloud sync for the App in iOS Settings at any time.
  • To ask about or delete analytics data linked to your device, email us at the address below.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will appear on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
