Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2025

Overview

Sticky Notes ("the App") is a simple note-taking application that respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Information We Collect

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Note Content: Text you enter in your notes
  • Note Metadata: Creation dates, edit dates, color preferences, archive status, and tag assignments
  • Tags: Custom organization tags with icons and titles
  • App Preferences: Theme colors and display settings

How We Use Your Information

All data is used solely to:

  • Display and organize your notes
  • Sync your notes across your devices via iCloud
  • Provide widget functionality on your home screen
  • Preserve your customization preferences

Data Storage and Sync

  • Local Storage: All data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData
  • iCloud Sync: If enabled on your device, notes automatically sync to your private iCloud container
  • Third-Party Access: We do not have access to your data. Your notes remain in your private iCloud storage
  • App Group Container: Used only to share data between the main app and widgets on your device

Third-Party Services

The App uses:

  • CloudKit: Apple's iCloud service for syncing (governed by Apple's Privacy Policy)
  • CloudKitSyncMonitor: An open-source library to monitor sync status (no data collection)

We do not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Third-party tracking tools
  • User behavior monitoring

Data Sharing

We do not:

  • Sell your data
  • Share your data with third parties
  • Transmit your data to our servers (we don't have any)
  • Access your note content

Data Retention

  • Notes remain on your device until you delete them
  • Archived notes persist until manually deleted
  • iCloud sync follows Apple's retention policies
  • Uninstalling the App removes local data; iCloud data persists per Apple's policies

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete any note at any time
  • Disable iCloud sync in iOS Settings
  • Export your data (via device backup)
  • Permanently delete the App and all local data

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information and is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected with a new "Last Updated" date.
